# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a place where careful work gets careful attention.

## Expected

- Be kind and specific. Criticize the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask before you assume otherwise.
- Give credit, cite sources and say what you measured.
- Accept that a forecast can be wrong and that saying so early is a service.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Pressure to hide a limitation or to overstate a result.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions and block participants who break these rules. Report a
problem to liyingjie@journey-ai.net. Reports are handled in confidence.
