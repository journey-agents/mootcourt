## Summary
The paper releases a benchmark and protocol for forecasting the outcome of a proposed A/B test from a ledger of concluded tests before launch: a ledger format with admission as a status, a rolling-origin backtest over versioned snapshots with a planned-exposure standard error and a leakage audit that returns zero violations on all three archives (leakage-audit-upworthy.json, -asos.json, -synthetic.json: fit_and_scored 0, fit_after_origin 0, outside_window 0, s_star_mismatch 0 for every rung), proper scores with floors and ceilings, calibration against a clustered null and within precision strata, gates by test at the archive's own base rate, and decision value against an exact random order and against a random order matched on the number of arms. The baseline suite is a ladder (P0 base rate, P1 empirical-Bayes Student-t prior, P2 kernel-weighted precedents behind an admission guard, now selected by forward chaining, four abstention triggers and a frozen card) and three recalibrated challengers on matched splits, run on the Upworthy exploratory split (3,797 scored tests, 13,247 contrasts, nine quarterly origins), the ASOS dataset (78 experiments) and a synthetic generator with known truth, every number drawn from paper/data/run7-tie-rank, one code state. The headline is a powered negative: P1 improves the log score over P0 by 0.149 nats (tables.aggregate cell=ALL rung=P1 logscore minus 0.8566 against rung=P0 minus 1.0059) and covers 81 and 89 percent pooled, yet its Brier skill against the base rate is 0.0015 (minus 0.0018 to 0.0048) on 3,797 tests against a release gate of 250 (gates.json by_base_rate 0.09 by_target 0.90 n_400 250), its ECE sits at the 100th percentile of the clustered null, and its 90 percent coverage runs from 1.000 to 0.655 across deciles of realized over planned standard error; P2, held in P1's family, equals P1 on skill and on the log score (logscore_vs_p1 0.000447, minus 0.000297 to 0.001191), the admitted pairs' realized difference splits into a location part of plus 0.0036 and a scale part of minus 0.0026, and the positive control admits a planted one-percent lift in 97 percent of cells at the size of the largest cell and recovers 0.180 (0.164 to 0.197) of the oracle's gain. Ranking by win probability beats the arm-count-matched random order for the text classifier (diff_matched 0.039459, adjusted 0.015752 to 0.063166) and the boosted model, is unresolved after adjustment for the precedent rung and the local set, and equals the baseline by construction for P0 and P1; the deployment-shaped archive returns undecidable at every gate. Every blocking and major finding of round 01 is closed at this commit, the repository's own audits pass (claims audit exit 0 with no failing row; number audit 734 numbers, 0 unmatched; 122 tests of the claims, sources and writing-rules suites pass), and what remains in my area is minor: the reading rule of the fifth hypothesis was changed after the runs and the paper does not say so beside the rule, the limitations section omits a few argued and open items, the abstract is 405 words and the results still stack numbers the tables hold, the artifact paragraph does not say who maintains the benchmark, and the round-01 letter carries statements the later design calls have overtaken.
