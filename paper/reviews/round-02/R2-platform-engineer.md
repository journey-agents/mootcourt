## Summary (five sentences, in the reviewer's own words, of what the paper claims and shows)
The paper releases a benchmark and protocol for forecasting the outcome of an A/B test before it launches from a ledger of concluded tests: a visibility rule keyed on each test's registered horizon, a planned-exposure standard error, a feature allow-list, proper scores with floors and ceilings, a calibration null clustered by test, gates by test at the archive's base rate, a leakage audit and decision value against a random order matched on the number of arms. On the headline archive (backtest-upworthy-20261103-4bfb354b87e3.json: header.tests_scored 3,797 over header.origins_scored 9) the empirical-Bayes prior improves the log score over the base rate by 0.149 nats (tables.aggregate cell ALL, P1 logscore minus 0.8566 against P0 minus 1.0059) with a Brier skill of 0.001473 (minus 0.001818 to 0.004798) against a release gate of 250 tests, and the precedent rung behind the admission guard equals it (P2 brier_skill 0.001898, logscore_vs_p1 0.000447, minus 0.000297 to 0.001191), while the positive control shows the guard admitting a planted lift at the size of the largest cell (paper-extras-synthetic positive_control_sensitivity n_tests 11100, strength 1.0: share_admitted 0.972, recovered_fraction 0.180). The operational half is a frozen card keyed on the ledger row, a weekly join that scores one pre-launch card per row with the card's own predictive (an abstained card on the forecast it withheld) and records in-flight and post-hoc cards apart, a nightly fit on the rows usable at its clock, a champion refresh by newer data and a paired, gated promotion of a different method; the replay of the archive under the loop's rules reports 13,247 cards over 111 weeks, 1,457 abstentions, coverage 0.8949 of 11,790 issued outcomes and 4 flagged weeks, all in the smallest cell from 2015-04-20 (replay-upworthy-20261103-4bfb354b87e3.json header and totals). The deployment-shaped archive is undecidable at every gate and the prior is overconfident there (backtest-asos-20261103-6bb5a15c5ff0.json tables.aggregate cell ALL, P1 brier_skill minus 0.1073, ece_percentile 98.8), and the exposure stand-in's leak is measured per archive (corr_log_impressions_abs_z 0.0994 on 13,453 rows against 0.2152 on 381). My trace of a card from issue to outcome on a clean clone reproduces every scored outcome's log score and base-rate score from the frozen card alone at the frozen planned standard error, the six round 01 gaps in the loop are closed in the code, and what remains is a card that the join will score against a ledger row of another cell, a backtest line that prints the pooled-dependence verdict for the smallest cell and comes after the window line, and four documentation items.
