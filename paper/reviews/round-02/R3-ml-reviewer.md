## Summary
The paper releases a benchmark and protocol for pre-launch forecasts of experiment outcomes: a rolling-origin backtest over versioned snapshots with a leakage guard and a post-hoc audit, proper scores with floors and ceilings, a clustered calibration null, gates counted in tests at the archive's base rate, and decision value against an exact random order and against a random order matched on the number of arms, run on the headline archive (13,247 scored contrasts, 3,797 tests), the deployment-shaped archive (337 contrasts, 68 experiments) and a synthetic generator with known truth. Its baseline suite is a ladder (P0 the empirical base rate, P1 a pooled Student-t empirical-Bayes prior, P2 kernel-weighted pairwise precedents in P1's family behind a two-standard-error admission guard, its weight and bandwidth now chosen by forward chaining) and three challengers (a precision-weighted, time-searched boosted model, a local empirical-Bayes comparison set and a pairwise TF-IDF logistic classifier), each raw and Platt-recalibrated on visible rows, scored on identical rows and tests. On the headline archive P1 beats P0 by 0.149 nats with a Brier skill of 0.0015 (minus 0.0018 to 0.0048); P2 is level with P1 (plus 0.0004 nats paired, minus 0.0003 to 0.0012; a weight admitted in 8 of 27 cell-origin pairs, whose realized difference is plus 0.0010, minus 0.0007 to 0.0025, split into a location part of plus 0.0036 and a scale part of minus 0.0026); the recalibrated text classifier is the one rung whose unadjusted skill interval clears zero (0.0046, 0.0003 to 0.0087) and it captures 3.9 points more positive lift than the arm-count-matched random order (0.022 to 0.054; 0.016 to 0.063 adjusted). The positive control now reports recovery beside admission: at 7,400 rows per cell the guard admits a planted one-percent lift in 97 percent of cells, the rung recovers 0.18 of the oracle's gain pooled (0.16 to 0.20) and 0.10 in the cell shaped like the real headline-only cell, and the text classifier fails the control (Brier skill minus 0.017, minus 0.022 to minus 0.011). My reading is that every finding of my round-01 review was fixed in the code and the reports and is verified here against the run-7 files (the change of the selection default by my own recomputation on the per-row score files), every item of my rubric section passes, the numbers in my area match the files, and what remains are six minors of qualification and presentation.
