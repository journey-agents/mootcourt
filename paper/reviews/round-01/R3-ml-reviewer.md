## Summary
The paper releases a benchmark and protocol for pre-launch forecasts of experiment outcomes: a ledger with admission as a status, a rolling-origin backtest over nine quarterly origins with a leakage guard, proper scores with floors and ceilings, a clustered calibration null, gates in tests, and decision value against an exact random order, run on the headline archive (13,247 scored contrasts, 3,797 tests), the deployment-shaped archive (337 contrasts, 68 experiments) and a synthetic generator. Its baseline suite is a ladder (P0 the empirical base rate, P1 a pooled Student-t empirical-Bayes prior, P2 kernel-weighted pairwise precedents in P1's family behind a two-standard-error admission guard) and three challengers (a precision-weighted tuned boosted model, a local empirical-Bayes comparison set, a pairwise TF-IDF logistic classifier), each raw and Platt-recalibrated on visible rows, scored on identical rows and tests. On the headline archive P1 beats P0 by 0.149 nats at every origin but has a Brier skill of 0.0015 (minus 0.0018 to 0.0048); P2, admitted in 9 of 27 cell-origin pairs, equals P1 on skill and loses 0.004 nats, the nine admitted pairs losing 0.0067 nats (minus 0.0088 to minus 0.0047) out of time; the recalibrated text classifier is the one rung whose skill interval clears zero (0.0046, 0.0003 to 0.0087) and it captures 5.0 points more positive lift than random order (0.0332 to 0.0659), while the prior equals random order by construction. A positive control shows the guard admits a planted one-percent text lift in 89 percent of cells at the size of the largest cell and nothing without one, after that check exposed and fixed a defect in P1's period level. My reading is that the round-00 fixes in my area are real and verified in the code and the reports, but the negative result on precedents is still not isolated: its sign depends on the selection rule (the paper's own forward-chaining ablation puts P2 0.0004 nats above P1), its loss sits on the typical row of the admitted pairs rather than on a transfer failure, the discrimination table pools between-cell movement into every rung's AUC, the positive control measures the guard's admission but not how much of a planted signal the rung recovers, the learning curve's skill is measured against a baseline that moves with the history, and two table captions carry log scores from a superseded run.

## Findings
- [major] Abstract, Section 1 contribution 3, Section 6.2, Section 7, Section 9. The headline negative, "precedents equal the prior on skill and lose 0.004 nats", is a property of the leave-one-test-out selection rule that is the default (`config.P2_SELECTION_DEFAULT`), and the paper's own ablation reverses its sign under the rule the method notes call the principled one. `ablations/...--p2-selection-forward.json` gives P2 minus 0.8562 against P1 minus 0.8566 pooled (plus 0.0004), and in the headline-only cell P2 minus 0.8666 against P1 minus 0.8674 (plus 0.0008; Brier skill minus 0.0036 against minus 0.0043), where the main run has P2 minus 0.8749 (minus 0.0075): a swing of 0.008 nats in the largest cell, twice the headline loss, and larger than the width of the paired interval the paper uses to call the loss real (Table 24, minus 0.0088 to minus 0.0047). Under forward chaining the guard admits eight pairs and P2 is above P1 at six of the seven admitted headline origins (`tables.score_by_origin`: 2014-04-01 minus 0.8570 against minus 0.8669, 2015-01-01 minus 0.7280 against minus 0.7306, 2015-04-01 minus 0.6680 against minus 0.6707, 2014-07-01 minus 0.5844 against minus 0.5845, 2014-10-01 minus 0.6565 against minus 0.6569, 2013-04-01 minus 0.9402 against minus 0.9403) and below at 2013-10-01 (minus 1.0804 against minus 1.0640). `docs/method.md` call 66 states the rule for changing the default (the better rule's log-score advantage must clear zero in a 90 percent paired interval) and says the two rules were not re-tested with an interval after the guard was added. Fix: compute the paired forward-minus-leave-one-test-out difference by test with the guard on (the `compare_guard` machinery does this for guard on and off), publish Table 24 for the forward rule, and either change the default by call 66's own rule or state in the abstract and Section 9 that the precedent rung loses 0.004 nats under leave-one-test-out and nothing under forward chaining. Section 7's "a spread of 0.006 nats around the prior" presents this swing as noise while Section 6.2 reads a 0.0067 loss with a tight interval as a finding; the two readings cannot both stand.
- [major] Section 6.2, Table 24, `paper-extras-upworthy guard_pairs`. The realized loss in the admitted pairs is read as "in-selection gains do not transfer", but on the per-row file (`scores-upworthy-20261103-4bfb354b87e3.parquet`, rows of the pair, P2 log score minus P1 log score) it sits on the typical row, not on a few: P2 is better than P1 on 19.5 percent of the 1,550 rows at 2014-07-01 (median difference minus 0.0504, mean minus 0.0267) and 27.8 percent of the 1,544 rows at 2014-10-01 (median minus 0.0666, mean minus 0.0153), between 28 and 37 percent in five other pairs, and 75 percent only at 2013-10-01 (the one pair with a clear realized gain, plus 0.0109); on the decisive rows of the same pairs (|y| above 1.96 s) P2 gains, plus 0.0446 over 402 rows and plus 0.1327 over 415 rows. A rung that loses on the bulk and wins on the tails has a predictive that is too wide for the bulk, which points at the scale term v(x) = lambda (v_m + tau_res squared) plus (1 minus lambda) tau squared, with tau_res squared a moment estimate from leave-one-test-out residuals (`tables.p2_selection` tau_res2 0.5159 at 2014-07-01 and 0.4002 at 2014-10-01 against 0.3935 and 0.2400 under forward chaining), rather than at the precedent mean. Fix: decompose the realized difference on the admitted pairs' rows into a location part (P2's location with P1's scale) and a scale part (P1's location with P2's scale), report both beside Table 24, and state which one the negative result is about; if it is the scale, the honest sentence is that the precedent mean carries a little (the decisive rows, the within-cell AUC below, the decision value) and the moment estimate of the residual variance throws it away.
- [major] Section 6.2, Table 25, Section 5 (scores). The pooled AUC and Murphy resolution credit every rung with between-cell and between-origin movement of the base rate, so the table ranks rungs on a quantity the prior does not forecast within a cell. P0's `auc_event` is 0.5475 (0.5294, 0.5655) and its `resolution_event` 0.00050 is above P1's 0.00027 and P2's 0.00034 although P0's probability is one number per cell and origin; on the per-row file, the AUC computed within each (origin, cell) stratum and averaged over rows is 0.500 for P0 by construction, 0.530 for P1, 0.547 for P2, 0.548 for the boosted model, 0.538 for the local set, 0.5645 for the text classifier and 0.559 recalibrated, and within the headline-only cell by origin 0.500, 0.502, 0.531, 0.529, 0.563, 0.577 and 0.575 (the per-cell rows of `tables.aggregate` already show it: headline_only auc_event P0 0.5283, P1 0.5264, P2 0.5285, text_lr 0.5826, local_eb 0.5710). So inside the largest cell the prior ranks tests at chance for the significance event and the text models at 0.56 to 0.58, a wider gap than the pooled 0.553 against 0.582 the paper reads, and P1's within-stratum 0.530 comes from the planned standard error alone. Fix: report the within-(origin, cell) AUC (and the direction AUC the same way) in Table 25, drop the pooled resolution or compute it within strata with its bias stated per stratum, and reword "discrimination apart from skill is as thin as the Brier skills say" to what the within-cell numbers show.
- [major] Section 6.8, Table 18, Table 19, Appendix B, `positive-control/backtest-synthetic-20261104-8cc34d3af720.json`. The positive control shows the guard admits a planted signal; it does not show that the precedent rung recovers it, and the one realized number says it mostly does not. With a planted lift of 1.0 (a third of the prior standard deviation) the headline-only cell gives P2 minus 2.6338 against P1 minus 2.6348, a gain of 0.0010 nats, and Brier skill 0.0265 against 0.0251, while the forecaster that knows the signal word would gain about 0.011 nats per row (one half of the log of (9.25 plus 2.55) over (9 plus 2.55) with the generator's tau 3.0 and median s 1.5), so P2 recovers about a tenth of the planted information; in the same cell the boosted model reaches a Brier skill of 0.0435 and an AUC of 0.6197 against P2's 0.5904 and P1's 0.5858, so the challenger recovers several times more of the signal than the rung the control is for. The control runs on 4,000 contrasts, about 1,200 visible rows per cell at the last origin (`tables.p2_selection` n_rows 1,192 in the headline-only cell at 2015-01-01), while the admission curve goes to 7,400 rows per cell; the realized P2 minus P1 (and the challengers') at each strength and size is not reported, and the text classifier, the one challenger built for a text signal, is absent from the control (`header.command` names gbm and local_eb only). Without these a reader cannot separate "the real archive has no precedent signal" from "the rung cannot recover a signal of the size that would matter". Fix: for each row of Table 19 report the realized out-of-time P2 minus P1 and the gain of the signal-word oracle, add text_lr to the control, and state the recovered share at 7,400 rows per cell as a number.
- [major] Section 7 (learning curve), Table 46, `learning-curve-upworthy-20261103-4bfb354b87e3.json`. The curve's skill is measured against a P0 refit on the same subsample, so the baseline moves with the history and the curve measures the gap between two forecasters that both learn from it. The reading "P1 is higher, not lower, with 123 rows (0.068): more history does not add discrimination" is an artifact: P1's Brier improves with history (`rows` rung P1 brier 0.0554 at fraction 0.01 to 0.0538 at 1.0) and P0's improves more (0.0602 to 0.0555), so the skill falls from 0.0679 to 0.0295 because the base rate is learned, not because the prior is. Fix: report the absolute Brier (or the skill against the full-history base rate) beside the relative skill, and read the curve as how much history the base rate and the prior each need. Two further sentences in the paragraph do not match the file: "the challengers keep their pooled order" (at fraction 1.0 the local set is the best rung at 0.0366, 0.0062 to 0.0734, above P1's 0.0295 and P2's 0.0311, and the text classifier is at 0.0104, the reverse of the pooled order text_lr above P1 above local_eb), and "the curve crosses both gates" compares history rows converted to tests with gates that count scored tests (the scored set is fixed at 318 tests at every fraction), which are different quantities.

## Questions for the authors
1. Under call 66's own rule, what is the paired by-test interval of forward chaining minus leave-one-test-out on the P2 log score with the guard on, and if it clears zero, why does the default stay leave-one-test-out?
2. On the rows of the two largest admitted pairs (2014-07-01 and 2014-10-01, headline only), what does P2's location scored with P1's scale give, and P1's location with P2's scale? Which term carries the 0.0267 and 0.0153 nat losses?
3. At 7,400 rows per cell and a planted lift of one percent, what is the realized out-of-time P2 minus P1 in nats and what share of the signal-word oracle's gain is it? What does the text classifier do on the same ledgers?
4. Is tau_res squared, a moment estimate under a Student-t with three degrees of freedom, inflated by the tails, and would a marginal-likelihood estimate in P1's family change the admitted pairs' realized differences?
5. Within the headline-only cell at a fixed origin, is any of P1's event AUC (0.502 within origin) left once the planned standard error is removed from the probability, and does the paper want to claim any within-cell discrimination for the prior at all?
6. Were the ablation captions written from run3 and left when the scoring changed, and is there any other number in the paper that `build_tables.py` does not generate?
