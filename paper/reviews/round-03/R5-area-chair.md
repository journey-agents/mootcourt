## Summary
The paper releases a benchmark and protocol for forecasting the outcome of a proposed A/B test from a ledger of concluded tests before launch: a ledger format with admission as a status, a rolling-origin backtest over versioned snapshots with a planned-exposure standard error and a leakage audit that returns zero violations on all three archives (leakage-audit-upworthy.json, -asos.json, -synthetic.json: fit_and_scored 0, fit_after_origin 0, outside_window 0, s_star_mismatch 0 for every rung), proper scores with floors and ceilings, calibration against a clustered null and within precision strata, gates by test at the archive's own base rate, and decision value against an exact random order and against a random order matched on the number of arms. The baseline suite is a ladder (P0 base rate, P1 empirical-Bayes Student-t prior, P2 kernel-weighted precedents behind an admission guard selected by forward chaining, four abstention triggers and a frozen card) and three recalibrated challengers on matched splits, run on the headline archive's exploratory split (3,797 scored tests, 13,247 contrasts, nine quarterly origins), the deployment-shaped archive (78 experiments) and a synthetic generator with known truth, every number drawn from paper/data/run7-tie-rank. The headline is a powered negative: P1 improves the log score over P0 by 0.149 nats (tables.aggregate cell=ALL rung=P1 logscore minus 0.8566 against rung=P0 minus 1.0059) yet its Brier skill against the base rate is 0.0015 (minus 0.0018 to 0.0048) on 3,797 tests against a release gate of 250 (gates.json by_base_rate 0.09 by_target 0.90 n_400 250), its ECE sits at the 100th percentile of the clustered null, and its 90 percent coverage runs from 1.000 to 0.655 across deciles of realized over planned standard error; P2, held in P1's family, is no better than P1 on skill (0.0019 against 0.0015) and within its interval on the log score (logscore_vs_p1 0.000447, minus 0.000297 to 0.001191), the positive control admits a planted one-percent lift in 97 percent of cells at the size of the largest cell and recovers 0.180 (0.164 to 0.197) of the oracle's gain pooled and 0.0995 in the cell shaped like the real one, and ranking by win probability beats the arm-count-matched random order for the text classifier (diff_matched 0.039459, adjusted 0.015752 to 0.063166) and the boosted model, is unresolved after adjustment for the precedent rung and the local set, and cannot rank within a cell for P0 and P1. Round 02's eight findings in my area are closed at this commit (seven fixed, one argued and accepted), the repository's own audits pass from a clean clone (claims audit exit 0 on the 128-row register; number audit 776 numbers, 0 unmatched, 108 sources resolved; the claims, sources and writing-rules suites pass), the abstract is 250 words with the negative first and the decision tables are legible at natural width.
