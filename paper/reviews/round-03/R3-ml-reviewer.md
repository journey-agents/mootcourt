## Summary
The paper releases a benchmark and protocol for pre-launch forecasts of experiment outcomes: a rolling-origin backtest over versioned snapshots behind a leakage guard and a post-hoc audit, proper scores with floors and ceilings, a clustered calibration null, gates counted in tests at the archive's base rate, and decision value against an exact random order and against a random order matched on the number of arms, run on the headline archive (13,247 scored contrasts in 3,797 tests), the deployment-shaped archive (78 experiments) and a synthetic generator with known truth. Its baseline suite is the ladder (P0 the empirical base rate, P1 a pooled Student-t empirical-Bayes prior, P2 kernel-weighted pairwise precedents in P1's family behind a two-standard-error admission guard, its weight and bandwidth chosen by forward chaining) and three challengers (a precision-weighted, time-searched boosted model, a local empirical-Bayes comparison set and a pairwise TF-IDF logistic classifier), each raw and Platt-recalibrated on visible rows, every rung scored on identical rows and tests. On the headline archive P1 beats P0 by 0.149 nats with a Brier skill of 0.0015 (minus 0.0018 to 0.0048); P2 is within the interval of P1 (plus 0.0004 nats paired, minus 0.0003 to 0.0012; forward chaining beats the leave-one-test-out rule of the first round by plus 0.0044 nats, 0.0031 to 0.0057, now a shipped key); the recalibrated text classifier is the one rung whose unadjusted skill interval clears zero (0.0046, 0.0003 to 0.0087) and it captures 3.9 points more positive lift than the arm-count-matched random order (0.022 to 0.054; 0.016 to 0.063 adjusted). The positive control reports admission and recovery side by side: at 7,400 rows per cell the guard admits a planted one-percent lift in 97 percent of cells, the rung recovers 0.18 of the oracle's gain pooled (0.16 to 0.20) and 0.10 in the cell shaped like the real headline-only cell, and the text classifier fails the control (Brier skill minus 0.017, minus 0.022 to minus 0.011). My reading is that all six of my round 02 findings are closed in the sources and the files, the matched splits and the ladder identity hold on the per-row files from a clean clone, twelve of my fourteen rubric items pass, and two fail: one learning-curve raw Brier score in Section 7 prints 0.0539 where the file holds 0.053846 and the table prints 0.0538 (a double rounding of the class the register's own note lists eight cases of; blocking by the rubric's definition and a one-digit fix), and the pooled realized difference of the admitted pairs prints with two intervals in two tables (minor).
