## Summary (five sentences, in the reviewer's own words, of what the paper claims and shows)
The paper releases a benchmark and protocol for forecasting the outcome of an A/B test before it launches from a ledger of concluded tests: a rolling-origin backtest over content-addressed snapshots, proper scores with floors and ceilings, a clustered calibration null, by-test gates from a power simulation at the archive's base rate, a shipped leakage audit and decision value against an exact random order matched on the number of arms, with a ladder of three rungs and three recalibrated challengers as the baseline suite on the Upworthy exploratory split, the ASOS dataset and a synthetic generator. Its headline result is a powered negative: on the headline archive the prior scores minus 0.8566 nats against the base rate's minus 1.0059 (`backtest-upworthy-20261103-4bfb354b87e3.json tables.aggregate[cell=ALL]`, 0.149 nats better) while its Brier skill is 0.001473 (minus 0.001818 to 0.004798) on 3,797 tests against a gate of 250 (`gates.json by_base_rate 0.09 by_target 0.90 n_400`), its calibration error sits at the 100th percentile of the clustered null, its coverage runs from 1.000 to 0.655 across deciles of realized over planned standard error, and precedents held in the same family are no better than it on skill (0.001898 against 0.001473) and sit at plus 0.000447 nats (minus 0.000297 to 0.001191) on the paired rows, the admitted pairs' pooled difference now read at 500 resamples like every other interval (plus 0.000969, minus 0.000730 to 0.002453, `paper-extras-upworthy guard_pairs.summary`). A text classifier on TF-IDF features captures 0.039459 (adjusted 0.015752 to 0.063166) more of the positive lift than the arm-count-matched random order at a quarter of the tests, the guard admits a planted one-percent lift in 0.972 of cells at the size of the largest cell and the rung recovers 0.180 (0.164 to 0.197) of the oracle's gain pooled and 0.099 (0.076 to 0.131) in the cell shaped like the real one, the fourth hypothesis is now read on the Brier skill it names (the issued rows minus 0.001781, the abstained rows 0.019853, `issued_row_brier_skill.rungs.P2`), and the deployment-shaped archive is "undecidable at this n" at every gate. The paper claims that every number traces to a report under `paper/data/run7-tie-rank`, that every table and figure regenerates from those reports, that the download refuses the archive's restricted splits, that the whole pipeline runs in 2,024 seconds on a laptop, and now what the 23,088 seconds of stages outside that run cost.
