## Summary (five sentences, in the reviewer's own words, of what the paper claims and shows)
The paper releases a benchmark and protocol for forecasting the outcome of an A/B test before it launches from a ledger of concluded tests: a visibility rule keyed on each test's registered horizon, a planned-exposure standard error, a feature allow-list, proper scores with floors and ceilings, a calibration null clustered by test, gates by test at the archive's base rate, a leakage audit and decision value against a random order matched on the number of arms. On the headline archive (backtest-upworthy-20261103-4bfb354b87e3.json: header.tests_scored 3,797 over header.origins_scored 9, header.scored_rows 13,247) the empirical-Bayes prior improves the log score over the base rate by 0.149 nats (tables.aggregate cell ALL: P1 logscore minus 0.8566 against P0 minus 1.0059) with a Brier skill of 0.001473 (minus 0.001818 to 0.004798) against a release gate of 250 tests (gates.json by_base_rate 0.09, by_target 0.90, n_400 250), and the precedent rung behind the admission guard is no better than it (P2 brier_skill 0.001898, minus 0.001752 to 0.005367; logscore_vs_p1 0.000447, minus 0.000297 to 0.001191), while the positive control shows the guard admitting a planted lift at the size of the largest cell (paper-extras-synthetic positive_control_sensitivity, n_tests 11100, strength 1.0: share_admitted 0.972, recovered_fraction 0.180, 0.164 to 0.197). The operational half is a frozen card keyed on the ledger row, a weekly join that scores one pre-launch card per row with the card's own predictive, records in-flight, post-hoc, reissued and, since this round, cell-mismatched cards apart and never scores them, a nightly fit on the rows usable at its clock, a champion refresh by newer data and a paired, gated promotion of a different method; the replay of the archive under the loop's rules reports 13,247 cards over 111 weeks, 1,457 abstentions, coverage 0.8949 on 11,790 issued outcomes and 4 flagged weeks (replay-upworthy-20261103-4bfb354b87e3.json header.n_weeks, totals.cards_issued, totals.abstained, totals.coverage_90_all, totals.coverage_90_all_n, totals.n_flagged_weeks). The deployment-shaped archive is undecidable at every gate and the prior is overconfident there (backtest-asos-20261103-6bb5a15c5ff0.json tables.aggregate cell ALL, P1 brier_skill minus 0.1073, ece_percentile 98.8, decidable_by_tests "undecidable at this n" on 68 scored tests), and the exposure stand-in's leak is measured per archive (paper-extras-asos corr_log_impressions_abs_z 0.2152 on 381 rows, corr_log_run_length_abs_z 0.1522, against 0.0994 on 13,453). My trace from a clean clone reproduces every scored outcome's log score and base-rate score from the frozen card alone at the frozen planned standard error, closes all seven items of my round 02 review, and leaves two rubric items failing: the new R2.15 (a card for a row the ledger already holds is accepted at issue on the command and the service, and the timing rule does not say which start it reads on an archive whose arms start at different times) and R2.14 (the two deployment-shaped display fields stay at the top level by decision, which I accept).
