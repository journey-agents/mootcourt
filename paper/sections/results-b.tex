P0's trailing base rate lags that fall, since it is the rate over rows already visible. The drift rule cannot see it either. The backtest reads each cell's reliability report once a quarter, at the origin. In use, the package's loop runs weekly (Section~\ref{sec:method}). The weekly replay (Table~\ref{tab:replay}, Appendix~\ref{app:tables}) runs that loop over the archive under its own rules. It issues one scored card per admitted row at the start of the week in which the test starts, from the bundle fitted at the latest quarterly origin on or before that week. It joins each outcome at the first weekly join on or after the date the result became usable, the test's assigned end on this archive (Section~\ref{sec:setting}), scores it with the card's own predictive (an abstained card on the forecast it withheld) and applies the drift rule at every join.

The positive control plants a text lift of 1.0 percent between headlines with and without a signal word, a third of the prior standard deviation in the headline-only cell. It runs under two designs, read apart in Table~\ref{tab:fmt-control} (from Tables~\ref{tab:positive-control}, \ref{tab:positive-control-gain} and~\ref{tab:positive-control-rungs}, Appendix~\ref{app:tables}). The rolling backtest is the design the real archive gets. The guard \emph{admits} a cell, or a cell and origin, when the precedent weight it selects there is above zero.
