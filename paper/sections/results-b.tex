\subsection{Drift and the weekly replay}
\label{sec:drift-results}
Table~\ref{tab:fmt-drift-year} condenses Table~\ref{tab:drift} (Appendix~\ref{app:tables}) by the calendar year in which the test started.
The skill does not decline with time, and the 2015 interval clears zero unadjusted and not after adjustment.
The significant-positive rate falls, a shift that P0's trailing base rate lags and that the drift rule, which watches the log score against P0 alone, cannot see.

The weekly replay (Table~\ref{tab:replay}, Appendix~\ref{app:tables}) runs the released loop under its own rules (one scored card per admitted row, issued at the week start, at or before the test's start) and one convention it fixes (a bundle refit at every quarterly origin).
It joins outcomes at the first weekly join on or after the horizon, scores each with the card's own predictive (abstained cards on the withheld forecast) and evaluates the drift rule weekly.
