The backtest reads each cell's reliability report once a quarter, at the origin. In use, the package's loop runs weekly (Section~\ref{sec:method}). The weekly replay (Table~\ref{tab:replay}, Appendix~\ref{app:tables}) runs that loop over the archive under its own rules. It issues one card per scored row, that is, per admitted row whose test starts at or after the first origin, at the start of the week in which the test starts, from the bundle fitted at the latest quarterly origin on or before that week. It joins each outcome at the first weekly join on or after the date the result became usable, the test's assigned end on this archive (Section~\ref{sec:setting}), scores it with the card's own predictive (an abstained card on the forecast it withheld) and applies the drift rule at every join.

The positive control plants a text lift of 1.0 percent between headlines with and without a signal word, a third of the prior standard deviation in the headline-only cell. The signal is planted in every cell of the ledger, and it is a larger share of the spread in the other two cells, one half and two thirds, because their spreads are smaller. The headline-only cell is read apart below because hypothesis H1 names it and because its scored rows match the real cell's in number. The size of the lift was fixed when the control was designed, before the control's first results and after results on the headline archive had been seen, and Table~\ref{tab:positive-control-gain} (Appendix~\ref{app:tables}) gives every size run. The control runs under two designs, read apart in Table~\ref{tab:fmt-control} (from Tables~\ref{tab:positive-control}, \ref{tab:positive-control-gain} and~\ref{tab:positive-control-rungs}, Appendix~\ref{app:tables}). The end-of-ledger split holds out a random 15 percent of a ledger's tests, fits on the rest as of the ledger's end, and repeats this over twelve ledgers drawn with different seeds. Its admission column is from a fit on the whole ledger, and its gains are from the fit without the held-out tests, which admits in fewer cells. The rolling backtest is the design the real archive gets. The guard \emph{admits} a cell, or a cell and origin, when the precedent weight it selects there is above zero. Each recovered figure is a fraction of an oracle's gain in log score over P1, not a fraction of the lift. The oracle is P1 fit with the planted term subtracted from every outcome and added back to each row's forecast, so it knows the signal exactly.

The text classifier is the rung that ranks best on the real archive, and here it fails the control on skill and ranks below the prior. The control therefore neither validates nor explains its value on the real archive. It shows only that this classifier does not read a planted location shift of this size on a synthetic ledger, so what it reads on the real archive is left open. The planted lift is small enough that even a model told the signal gains little, and no run plants a larger lift for the classifier, so the failure may reflect the size of the planted signal as much as the classifier. It is not evidence that the classifier's ranking on the real archive is spurious, and one exploratory run on one archive is all the evidence for that ranking.
