\paragraph{Tooling landscape.}
\label{sec:landscape}
A fetch-verified survey of thirteen commercial experimentation platforms, seventeen open-source projects, sixteen internal platforms described in public papers and the 2023 to 2026 simulation and language-model tools found no product that issues a pre-launch outcome distribution or win probability for a proposed experiment, abstains, scores its forecasts against live results, or backtests over an archive; the three 2026 screening systems cited above are the nearest, and Appendix~\ref{app:landscape} with Table~\ref{tab:landscape} gives the survey and its gap table.
