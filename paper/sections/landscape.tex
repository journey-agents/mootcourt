Our survey also covered four groups of tools: commercial experimentation platforms, open-source projects, internal platforms described in public papers, and the simulation and language-model tools of 2023 to 2026. Each entry was checked against its own public pages. We found none that does all four of these things. Doing all four means issuing a pre-launch outcome distribution or win probability for a proposed experiment, abstaining, scoring its forecasts against the results of real tests, and backtesting over an archive. The three screening systems above come nearest on the learned prior, and some simulation tools do so on a pre-launch distribution with a form of abstention. None combines them (Appendix~\ref{app:landscape}, Table~\ref{tab:landscape}).
