\subsection{Tooling landscape}
\label{sec:landscape}
A fetch-verified survey of thirteen commercial experimentation platforms, seventeen open-source projects, sixteen internal platforms described in public papers, and the 2023 to 2026 wave of simulation and language-model tools (released with the code as \texttt{docs/landscape.md}) found no product that issues a pre-launch outcome distribution or win probability for a proposed experiment, abstains, scores its forecasts against live results, or runs a backtest over an experiment archive. What exists are the parts: power calculators fed by historical variance in nearly every platform; hand-typed informative priors whose documentation suggests past-experiment averages; searchable experiment knowledge bases in six commercial products and one open-source one; a single platform-wide learned prior pooled across customers; two archive-trained ranking scores for ideas and ad creatives, one of them a vendor claim without a published validation; and simulation agents whose directional backtests on a few dozen tests are the only validations reported.
