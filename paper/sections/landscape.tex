We also surveyed four groups of tools: commercial experimentation platforms, open-source projects, internal platforms described in public papers, and the simulation and language-model tools of 2023 to 2026. We checked each entry against its own public pages. We found none that does all four of these: issues a pre-launch outcome distribution or win probability for a proposed experiment, abstains, scores its forecasts against the results of real tests, and backtests over an archive. The three screening systems above come nearest (Appendix~\ref{app:landscape}, Table~\ref{tab:landscape}).
