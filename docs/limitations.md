# Limitations

- **The scores are against the observed estimate, not the unknown true effect.** A predictor is judged on the estimate a test
  produced, noise included, so no predictor can reach a perfect score and a small gain is hard to see.
- **A forecast is only as good as the ledger.** A small ledger, a ledger of one kind of test or a ledger whose tests are
  unlike the proposal gives a weak or abstained card, and the card says so.
- **Gates decide what can be compared, not what is useful.** An undecidable comparison is not a negative result.
- **Precedent is not mechanism.** The model says what happened in tests that looked like this one. It does not say why.
- **Public archives are not your program.** The reference runs use the Upworthy Research Archive and the ASOS dataset to show
  the method and its limits. Their numbers say nothing about another organization's tests.
- **A language-model agent may have seen a public archive.** Origins before the agent's knowledge cutoff are flagged as
  possibly contaminated, and a weight is earned on clean origins unless the run says otherwise.
- **The service has no authentication.**

The [method page](method.md) lists the simplifications to read before trusting a number and every call that was made when
the method was built.
